## Mission

GitHubDaily will push a batch of excellent open source projects from GitHub to developers at irregular times every day, helping developers discover the most popular open source projects, enabling them to keep their fingers on the pulse of technology, expand their technical horizons, and improve their technical skills through learning from open source projects.

- Weibo: [@GitHubDaily](https://weibo.com/GitHubDaily)
- Zhihu: [@GitHubDaily](https://www.zhihu.com/people/githubdaily)

> Welcome to [Issues](https://github.com/AtulWanve/GitHubDaily/issues/new) to recommend or self-recommend projects, so more people can know about your project.
